Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

LiyaJin School Uploader ("the tool") is an internal tool used only by the LiyaJin School team to upload videos to the team's own TikTok account (@liyajin.school) as drafts.

What we access

  • Basic TikTok profile information of the authorizing account (open ID, display name, avatar) — to confirm the correct account is connected.
  • Permission to upload video drafts to that account's TikTok inbox (video.upload).

What we store

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored only on the team's own computer and are never shared. We do not collect, sell or share personal data of any other TikTok users.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

Instagram (LiyaJin Data Platform)

LiyaJin Data Platform is an internal tool used only by the LiyaJin School team for the team's own Instagram professional account (@liyajin.school). It reads comments on our own posts and messages sent to our account so that our team can reply to questions about lessons. Replies are written and sent by a person on our team.

  • What we access: the account's profile and posts, comments on those posts, and direct messages in the account's inbox, through the Instagram API with Instagram Login.
  • What we store: the access token and copies of comments and messages, only on the team's own computer, for up to 90 days. We do not sell or share this data and do not use it for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time in Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites → Remove.

Data deletion

If you wrote a comment or message to @liyajin.school and want us to delete our stored copy, send a direct message to @liyajin.school or an email to liyajincouple@gmail.com with your Instagram username and the words "delete my data". We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply.
